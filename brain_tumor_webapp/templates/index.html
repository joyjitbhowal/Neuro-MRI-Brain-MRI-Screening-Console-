About

What this tool does

A computer-vision ensemble that gives a second, data-driven look at a brain MRI — in plain terms.

01 · The task

Four-class MRI classification

Each scan is classified into one of four outcomes: glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor, or no tumor. Every model votes independently; the page combines their votes into a single consensus verdict, plus a probability-averaged ensemble result.

02 · The training data

Trained on 5,941 clinical scans

T1-weighted contrast-enhanced MRI images sourced from Epic & CSCR Hospital, Bangladesh, split 70 / 15 / 15 into train / validation / test — with an automated check confirming zero overlap between the splits, so the numbers on this page reflect genuine generalization, not memorized duplicates.

5,941 images 4 classes T1W-CE zero-overlap split
03 · The models

Three training paradigms

The seven models compare three approaches: architectures trained from scratch on this dataset, pretrained ImageNet backbones used with frozen weights, and pretrained backbones fine-tuned on MRI. The benchmark section shows exactly how that choice plays out.

from scratch frozen pretrained fine-tuned
